Our story

Fewer things,
made properly.

Started in 2019 with one serum and a handloom co-operative in Bhagalpur. Still one house, still small batch.

2019 — now

We make six things. That is deliberate.

Most beauty houses launch forty SKUs a year. We launch four. Every formula is developed with our own lab partners, tested on Indian skin in Indian humidity, and reformulated until it earns a second order.

The atelier works the same way: 120 pieces per style with weaver co-operatives in Bhagalpur and Jaipur, and bags built to survive a Mumbai monsoon.
